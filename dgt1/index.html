Dynamical Group Theory I · 2023

The 1st Korea-Chile Workshop on Dynamical Group Theory

September 26 – 28, 2023·Sheraton Grand Incheon, Incheon, Korea

The meeting that started the series. Researchers from Korea and Chile came together around group actions, orderability, and low-dimensional dynamics, with a program spanning one-manifold dynamics, laminar group theory, hyperbolic geometry, and decision problems.

Talks9

  • Andrés Navas (Universidad de Santiago de Chile) Deformation of actions on 1-manifolds
  • Cristóbal Rivas (Universidad de Chile) Regular orders on groups
  • Inkang Kim (KIAS) Dynamics of convex projective structures: geometry of the Sinai–Ruelle–Bowen measure
  • Wenyuan Yang (Peking University) Generic 3-manifolds are hyperbolic
  • Hyungryul Baik (KAIST) Sol manifolds obtained from a veering pair of laminations
  • Sungwoon Kim (Jeju National University) Structural stability of meandering hyperbolic group actions
  • Carl-Fredrik Nyberg-Brodda Decision problems in Baumslag–Solitar-like monoids
  • KyeongRo Kim An overview of laminar group theory
  • Juhun Baik (KAIST) Monodromy through the bifurcation locus of the Mandelbrot set

Organizers

  • Sang-hyun Kim (KIAS)
  • Inhyeok Choi (KIAS)
