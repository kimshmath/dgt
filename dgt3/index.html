Dynamical Group Theory III · 2024

New Methods in Group Actions on Manifolds

August 13 – 16, 2024·KIAS Building 1, Room 1503, Seoul, Korea

A workshop on emerging techniques for studying group actions on manifolds, built around a mini-course and a compact program of invited talks, with generous time for discussion.
