Conference Series · 2023 — Present

Dynamical Group Theory

A series of workshops on group actions, dynamics, and geometry — bringing together researchers in geometric group theory, low-dimensional topology, and dynamical systems, at KIAS and partner institutions around the world.

Steered by Inhyeok Choi and Sang-hyun Kim (KIAS), with co-organizers in Korea, France, Chile, and the US.
