Dynamical Group Theory IV · 2024

KIAS-Rice Workshop on Geometric Topology

September 24 – 27, 2024·June E Huh Center for Mathematical Challenges, KIAS, Seoul, Korea

A joint KIAS–Rice meeting themed on geometric group theory and hyperbolic geometry, both in a very broad sense, with ample free time for discussion throughout the four days.
